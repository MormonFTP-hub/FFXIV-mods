How to play

Hit the dummy with your skills and watch the meter. The parse starts on your first hit and stops when the timer runs out.

Skills
Keys 1–0 for the top row and Q E F Z X C G T for the stigma row, or click or tap the buttons. Press a skill while another is still animating and it goes next.
Moving
W A S D or the arrow keys, or click or tap the ground. The shaded half behind the dummy is where back attacks land.
Procs
A skill with a gold ring is ready now (Heart Gore after a critical hit, Shadow Fall on its proc). Its ring drains as the window runs out.
Menu
C class, B build, V stats, L leaders, R reset, P auto-play, Esc closes a panel.
