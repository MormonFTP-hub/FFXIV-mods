Damage per second by enemy DEF

How this is worked out

ATK for the hit: floor(ATK × (1 + buffs%)) + flat ATK, then times the skill and extra multipliers.

Enemy DEF: (DEF − flat reduction) × (1 − reduction%), then the operator's own ignore: minus the flat part, then times (1 − ignore%). RES works the same way and stays between 0 and 100.

Physical hit: ATK − DEF. Arts hit: ATK × (1 − RES / 100). True damage skips both. A hit never does less than 5% of its ATK. Fragile multiplies the result, and only the strongest Fragile counts.

Time between attacks: (interval + interval change) × 100 / (100 + ASPD bonus). Total ASPD stays between 20 and 600.

Picked operators: ATK is the level's base ATK plus trust, potential and module bonuses, taken from the game's own data tables (global server). From a skill, the page fills in only its ATK bonus, damage multiplier, ASPD bonus, hit count and duration. Attack interval changes, talents and anything unusual are left to you, so read the skill text shown under the picker.

Rules are from the Arknights Terra Wiki damage pages. Enter several ATK or DEF percentages as one combined number.

Chance of the operator by pull count

How this is worked out

Each pull has a 2% chance of a 6★. After 50 pulls without one, the chance goes up by 2% on every pull: 4% on the 51st, 6% on the 52nd, and 100% on the 99th. Getting a 6★ resets the count.

When a 6★ appears, it is the one you want with the share shown for the banner type. The page follows every possible run of luck pull by pull and adds up the ones that end with the operator.

One pull costs 600 Orundum. Standard banners share one pity count; each limited banner keeps its own, so enter 0 for a limited banner you have not pulled on. The guarantee and the 300-pull trade are counted from your first pull on the banner.

Rates are from the Arknights Terra Wiki headhunting page. "Other pulls per month" starts at 5 as a rough figure for certificate-shop and sign-in permits; change it to what you really get.

          How this is worked out

          Power, operator slots and build costs for every room level come from the game's own data tables (global server). Build cost covers the rooms only: clearing space and the materials' own crafting costs are not counted.

          Factories: one Pure Gold takes 1 h 12 min, a Drill, Frontline or Tactical Battle Record takes 45 min, 1 h 20 min or 3 h (200, 400 or 1,000 EXP), and an Originium Shard takes 1 h. A productivity bonus of +100% doubles the speed.

          Picked operators: each one's Trading Post or Factory skills at the chosen promotion are added up, plus 1% per operator, and shared out evenly over the rooms of that kind. Skills that only work for one product count only there. Skills that depend on who else is in the room, on time, or on other rooms are marked, and the page takes the plain number from their text (the highest one for skills that build up over time), so correct those yourself. Control Center skills are not counted: add them in the typed-in bonus.

          Trading Posts: a level 3 post rolls a 2, 3 or 4 Pure Gold order (1,000, 1,500 or 2,000 LMD) 30%, 50% and 20% of the time, taking 2 h 24 min, 3 h 30 min and 4 h 36 min. Level 2 rolls 60% and 40% of the first two, and level 1 only the first. Tailoring skills shift a level 3 post toward 4 Pure Gold orders. An Orundum order takes 2 Originium Shards, pays 20 Orundum and takes 2 h. Order rules are from the Arknights Terra Wiki.

          Drones come back at 1 every 6 minutes (240 a day) and each one skips 3 minutes of work. Operator skills do not speed drones up. If the Trading Posts want more Pure Gold than the base makes, LMD is counted only for the Pure Gold there is.

          How this is worked out

          Sanity comes back at 1 every 6 minutes, which is 240 a day. The cap is set by your Doctor level: 135 at level 5, 165 at level 35, and 180 from level 117.

          Average runs: materials needed ÷ drop chance. The "9 times out of 10" line is the number of runs that is enough for 90% of players, since drops are luck. Sanity per material is stage cost ÷ drop chance; lower is better when you compare stages.
