How a GOATS fight on Hanamura A went

  1. Group up and walk in behind the shield. Six players move as one ball. Lúcio plays speed song on the walk, the Rein leads with Barrier Field up, Brig hugs the Rein, Zen stays a few metres back with line of sight.
  2. Poke at the gate. Both shields face each other through the main gate. D.Va and Zen poke, Zen discords a tank, Zarya fishes for energy. Nobody steps out alone: the side that over-extends dies first.
  3. The engage. When someone calls it, Lúcio hits Amp It Up with speed song and all six run in at once. The Rein drops shield only when he's on top of the enemy and swings, Brig whips and bashes, D.Va flies onto their Zen.
  4. The ult trade. The fight is decided by the order ults go out. A Graviton Surge baits a Transcendence or a Sound Barrier; the next big ult (a D.Va bomb into grav, or an Earthshatter) lands when they have no save left. Track what they've used: "Trans is down!" is the call that wins fights.
  5. Saves. Zarya bubbles absorb a Shatter or a bomb hit outright. Beat counters the bomb, Trans counters the sustained damage of a grav. Your Rein shield blocks a bomb and an enemy Shatter. Face it the right way.
  6. As Reinhardt: don't swing with your shield down in front of a Rein with Shatter; Shatter when their shield is down or turned, or right after their saves are gone; use Fire Strike through their shield when they bunch up; Charge only to pin an isolated Zen or Lúcio, never into five people.
