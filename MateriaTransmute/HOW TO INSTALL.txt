MATERIA TRANSMUTER
Gives your junk materia to Mutamix Bubblypots five at a time, so you get other materia back and sometimes a higher grade.

You need XIVLauncher with Dalamud, the launcher that runs plugins. If you already use any plugin, you have it.


=== EASY WAY (updates install themselves) ===

1. In game, type  /xlsettings  and open the "Experimental" tab.
2. Find "Custom Plugin Repositories". Paste this link into the empty box, then click the + next to it:

      https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MormonFTP-hub/FFXIV-mods/main/repo.json

3. Click "Save and Close" at the bottom.
4. Type  /xlplugins , search for "Materia Transmuter" and click Install.


=== USING THIS ZIP INSTEAD ===

1. Make a folder you won't delete, for example  C:\FFXIV Plugins\Materia Transmuter , and unzip this file into it.
2. In game, type  /xlsettings  and open the "Experimental" tab.
3. Under "Dev Plugin Locations", paste the full path to the DLL, for example
      C:\FFXIV Plugins\Materia Transmuter\MateriaTransmute.dll
   click the + next to it, then "Save and Close".
4. Type  /xlplugins , open "Dev Tools", then "Installed Dev Plugins", and turn on Materia Transmuter.


=== USING IT ===

1. Do the level 19 quest "Marvelously Mutable Materia" first if you haven't. It unlocks trading with Mutamix.
2. Type  /transmute . Click "Show on map" to flag Mutamix Bubblypots at the Bonfire in Central Thanalan
   (X 23.7, Y 13.6), then go there.
3. On the "Pick junk" tab, tick the materia you don't want. Ticking a stat's name, such as Piety, ticks every grade of it.
4. Stand next to Mutamix and press Start. It keeps trading until fewer than five of any grade are left.
   Press Stop, or type  /transmute stop , at any time.

Each trade takes five materia of the same grade. You get back one materia of a different stat, sometimes one grade higher.
The plugin only uses materia you ticked. "Settings" can keep a few of each, or stop after a set number of trades.
"Results" lists what every trade gave you.
